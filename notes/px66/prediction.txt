px66 — the partition table: the prediction, written before the first change
(2026-10-23, at pax trunk e5dfad45; Limine's USAGE.md and CONFIG.md for
v12.9.1 read, no code written, no fixture made, the guest not yet asked).

1. Kernel lines. GPT and the partition window together need about 450
   lines of wolf, comments included: kernel/block about 330 more (the
   protective MBR, both headers with their CRC32 and the entry array,
   the device records for the partitions, the window's offset and
   bound, /proc/partitions' text, the exclusive-mount check), kernel/
   fstab about 80 (PARTUUID=, UUID=, and LABEL=/PARTLABEL= if they are
   the same lookup), kernel/procfs about 25 (one entry). Of that, the
   window itself (offset and bound in `rw`) under 30.

2. The fixture the guest and my first reading disagree on. I expect to
   agree with the guest on the bad-primary fixture (both use the backup
   header). The first disagreement will be the device numbers past the
   fifteenth partition: Linux gives virtio-blk 16 minors a disk, so a
   table with more than 15 used entries puts vda16 and on somewhere
   else (I guess the extended major 259), and my first reading will
   number them 254:16 and on.

3. Limine's BIOS path on GPT needs a partition of its own: yes, a BIOS
   boot partition of at least 32 KiB whose number is passed to
   `limine bios-install` (USAGE.md "BIOS/GPT"). I guess the installer
   refuses a GPT disk when no partition is named, so R-px66-2 has one
   layout, not two.

4. Boot time, from QEMU's start to the kernel's first line: the disk
   within 25% of the ISO under TCG, BIOS and UEFI alike (Limine's
   load of the kernel and the initramfs is the cost either way, and
   SeaBIOS's virtio-blk int13 costs about what its AHCI CD-ROM read
   does); under KVM both under one second.

5. px63's halt already leaves the clean flag set (kernel/ext2's
   unmount sets VALID when the volume was clean at its mount), so row
   4 passes on the first run without a fix; QEMU killed mid-session
   leaves `not clean`, and the volume then stays not clean through
   later clean halts until e2fsck repairs it, which I expect Linux's
   guest to do too.
