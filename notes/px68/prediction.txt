px68 — the journal: the prediction, written before the first change
(2026-10-24, at pax trunk 54570380, px67's merge).

1. Kernel lines the journal takes. A module of its own (kernel/journal:
   the journal superblock, the log's runs over inode 8's blocks, the
   running transaction, the commit with its ordering and flushes, the
   checkpoint, revoke records, the replay's three passes): about 900
   lines with its header. kernel/ext2 about 200 more (has_journal and
   RECOVER at the mount, metadata marked apart from data, the unmount's
   order, the feature names), kernel/block about 120 (a buffer the
   running transaction holds is never written home or evicted; sync
   leaves it to the journal), kernel/tree, fstab and user about 40 (the
   flusher's turn and the call boundary commit), kmain about 30 (`init=`
   for the crash row's second boot). About 1,300 inserted in all.

2. The crash row after it (seed 67, 1,500 operations, 24 kills, each
   image booted by PAX and halted before the host reads it): counts 0,
   orphans 0, worse 0 on all 24; lost writes on about 20 (every kill
   that falls between two commits loses what came after the last one:
   up to the flusher's 5 s, tens to a few hundred operations), "none"
   on the rest (a kill just after a commit, or before the first
   operation reached the disk). No operation completed before the last
   commit block on the disk is lost, on any image.

3. The write session's cost with the journal: within 10% of px67's
   (30.09 s uncut on kasumi, TCG): each 5 s commit logs a few dozen
   metadata blocks (the directory's, the inode tables', two bitmaps, a
   descriptor, the superblock) and writes them home at the checkpoint,
   with three flushes a commit; about 1.05 times px67's. The halt about
   50 ms longer (the last commit and the checkpoint).

4. Replay time at boot: not visible to a person; under 50 ms of TCG on
   kasumi for the transactions a 5 s interval leaves (one to a few, a
   few dozen blocks each), measured from the serial's timestamps.

5. The oracle row that disagrees first: e2fsck -fn on PAX's first clean
   unmount of a journalled volume, about the journal superblock or the
   needs_recovery flag (a sequence, a start, or the flag left as e2fsck
   does not expect), before any replay row.
