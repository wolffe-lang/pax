px74 — prediction, recorded before implementation

QEMU: expect no USB legacy support extended capability on qemu-xhci;
absence should require no ownership handshake. If present, set OS-owned,
wait boundedly for BIOS-owned to clear, then disable legacy SMI enables.
First-key report-to-read latency: expect under 20 ms at an idle guest,
measured separately from boot/enumeration and host monitor injection.
Linux typematic: predict the contract's 250 ms delay, roughly 30 ms period
(33 cps); measure the guest rather than treating the prediction as evidence.
Two-deep hub: expect first attempt to need debugging of route nibbles and
transaction-translator parent fields; final gate must enumerate on first boot.

R-px74-1: recommend B (INTx). kernel/virtio explicitly leaves MSI-X alone;
kernel/pci and kernel/apic do not yet expose an MSI-X routing API.
R-px74-2: recommend enumerate/log but not drive SuperSpeed non-hub devices;
USB 2 boot keyboards and hubs remain in scope. No ruling decided here.
R-px74-3: campaign line 36 says px74 drives mass storage, but the lane
contract specifies keyboard/hubs and no block transport. Recommend keeping
mass storage in a separately contracted lane; the live initramfs does not
need to reread its boot stick to type. Orchestrator clarification required.
