No disk may be a target, so nothing will be written.
To allow a disk, add one line to /pax/install-targets on the boot stick's EFI partition,
from any OS, before booting the stick:
  model=<model> serial=<serial>
with the disk's model and serial exactly as Linux shows them:
  cat /sys/block/sdX/device/model /sys/block/sdX/device/serial
(the refusals above print each disk's own line to copy).
