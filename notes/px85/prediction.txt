The cause shared by pax#260 and pax#261 (from the run 41153232393 artifacts, read before this file):
the guest's clocks disagree about time that passed while its vCPU did not run. The HPET and the
PM timer are QEMU's virtual clock and keep counting through a host stall; the LAPIC tick is an
interrupt, and ticks that fall due while the vCPU is stalled coalesce into one. PAX's
CLOCK_MONOTONIC, poll's timeout and the xHCI software repeat all count ticks (kernel/user
`now_ns` = ticks x 10000151 ns; kernel/xhci `tick`). Attempt 2's 6 reports over a 1000 ms
wall-clock hold mean the guest counted about 40 ticks across that second.
