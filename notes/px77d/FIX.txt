WHAT CHANGED: the plain boot reset kasumi once in two tries (entry 11), the triage boot never.
The difference that can come and go: until now the plain kernel ran its first seconds with no
exception table and machine checks switched off, so an NMI or a machine check arriving then
reset the board silently. On kasumi those seconds are long: the boot draws its whole file
listing (about 340 lines) before loading the table, and every scroll reads the screen back
through the RTX 4090's PCIe BAR (about 6 GB of reads at 2560x1440). This kernel loads its
exception table and turns machine checks on as its very first act:
  * an NMI is now printed as a line starting "nmi: 1 at rip ..." and the boot carries on;
  * a machine check now prints "mce: bank ..." lines and "PANIC machine check ..." and stops
    there, on screen, instead of resetting.

ENTRY 1 (the default) boots by itself after 5 seconds: the live system with the fix.
Entries 2 and 3 are the triage, as on the triage stick, in case.
