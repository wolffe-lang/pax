px96 — the quiet console: the prediction, written 2026-11-04T15:06Z before the first change.

Base: trunk 44b44435.

The census (kernel/user/user.lu, kernel/process/process.lu, kernel/signal/signal.lu read
for every `user: <name> pid <n> …` line; kernel/files prints no line of its own):
  - `write fd F: -EBADF` (write and writev on a closed or read-only descriptor): printed
    whatever `quiet` says. No test asserts it.
  - `write fd F buf 0x<b> len L: -EFAULT` (a console write whose buffer is not readable):
    printed whatever `quiet` says. tests/mpx3-user U4 asserts three of them, booted without
    `quiet`.
  - `syscall N: -ENOSYS`: already gated on `quiet` (px15).
  - refusals by name (`syscall N: refused: <why>, -<errno>`, `syscall 16: refused: tty ioctl
    0x<r>, -ENOTTY`, `user: exec <path>: refused: <why>`): printed whatever `quiet` says, by
    px15's design; quiet suites (mpx3-shell S2 and S4, mpx4-session E1, mpx4-lobo L2) hold
    quiet boots to "no refusal", which means something only while refusals print. Predicted
    ruling: they stay (R-px96-1).
  - `killed:` (a fault or a signal that ends the process) and panics: not errno lines; they stay.

The change, predicted:
  1. user/programs.S: badptr also closes fd 2 and writes to it (-EBADF, 9), and when all four
     answers are Linux's it writes its own line on fd 1 and exits 14 as before.
  2. tests/mpx3-user: U4 takes the -EBADF line and badptr's own line; a fourth image,
     kmain_user booted with `cmdline: quiet` (tests/mpx3-user.d/limine-quiet.conf), adds
       U9  quiet: badptr's own line (its errnos came back) and no `write fd` line at all;
       U10 quiet keeps the rest: one `user: quiet` line, the five faults' `killed:` lines,
           no -ENOSYS and no exit line.
     At the rows' commit, before the gate: U0-U8 and U10 PASS, U9 FAILs naming the -EFAULT
     and -EBADF lines on the quiet image.
  3. kernel/user/user.lu: the two lines behind one gate, `errno_lines()` (false when the console
     is quiet), the way exec and exit are; the `user: quiet` line names them. Then U9 PASSes and
     nothing else moves.
  4. The plant, one hunk off the final head: `errno_lines()` answers true. U9 red by name
     (FAIL U9 … on every leg it boots), U4 and U10 still PASS.

The gauntlet at the final head (satoyama, both locks, slot A's CPUs): tests/ci-plan; tests/mpx3-user
on both tiers and both firmware; kmain_console built, native and release. Predicted all green.
