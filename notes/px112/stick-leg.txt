px112: burrowd on the stick's console, the metal leg, for the next stick built from a tree that carries px112.

Under QEMU the stick's shape is tests/mpx5-burrowd's B11 (the live entry as pid 1, the shipped configuration, no
/etc/shadow): burrowd's key made at boot, a connection from the LAN refused, burrowd answering on loopback. The
stick lane runs that suite beside its own before staging the stick.

On the metal target:

1. The cable in the I225-V's port, as px105's leg has it.
2. Boot the stick's default entry: the live console.
3. Before `PAX live medium: log in as root (no password).`, after the network's lines (px105's leg), the console
   prints, in this order:

     burrowd: host key 256 SHA256:<43 characters> pax (ED25519) (made at this boot)
     burrowd: started; its log is /var/log/burrowd.log

   (`pax` is the machine's host name; the fingerprint is new at every boot of the stick, since the stick keeps
   nothing.)
4. At `login:` type `root`, then the witness:

     echo PX112-BURROWD=begin
     burrow-keygen -l -f /etc/burrowd/ssh_host_ed25519_key
     grep ListenAddress /etc/burrowd/burrowd_config
     burrow-keygen -q -t ed25519 -N '' -f /tmp/px112-k
     ssh -i /tmp/px112-k -o BatchMode=yes -o StrictHostKeyChecking=accept-new -o UserKnownHostsFile=/tmp/px112-kh pax@127.0.0.1 true; echo ssh=$?
     cat /var/log/burrowd.log
     echo PX112-BURROWD=end

   The fingerprint is step 3's; the listen line is `ListenAddress 127.0.0.1`; ssh prints
   `pax@127.0.0.1: Permission denied (publickey,password).` and `ssh=255` (burrowd answered, and refused: the stick
   has no password and no key for pax); the log's first lines are `host key 256 SHA256:…` and `listening on
   127.0.0.1:22`, then one line `from UNKNOWN port 0 user pax key SHA256:… outcome auth_key_unlisted status -`
   (the address reads UNKNOWN on PAX until wolf-std can ask the kernel for a peer's).
5. From another machine on the LAN, `ssh pax@<the stick's address> true` answers `connect to host … port 22:
   Connection refused`: the stick listens on loopback alone (R-px112-9, C).
6. A photograph of the lines in 3 and 4, and the other machine's line in 5, is the record.

What each other answer means:
- `burrowd: /bin/burrowd or /bin/burrow-keygen is missing; not started`: the stick was built from a tree without
  px112's tools/mkburrow; nothing else of the leg runs.
- `burrowd: no host key could be made at …`: burrow-keygen could not write /etc/burrowd; photograph the line before
  it.
- No `burrowd:` line at all: the stick's /etc/pax-boot.d is missing, or its live entry is older than px112.
- ssh's `Connection refused` at step 4: burrowd is not listening; its start refusal is the log's last line
  (`burrowd: <name>: …`), and the loop tries again after a wait that doubles to five minutes.

An installed system (fresh, or upgraded from such a stick) listens on every address: the installer writes
`ListenAddress 0.0.0.0` into its /etc/burrowd/burrowd_config. Its first check from another machine is in the note
(notes/px112-burrowd-on-pax.md, "The metal target").
