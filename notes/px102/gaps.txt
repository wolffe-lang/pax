px102 — named blocked-call gaps: the prediction, written 2026-11-06 before
the witnesses have run anywhere. Sources: ptrace(2), signal(7)'s
"Interruption of system calls", restart_syscall(2), futex(2), recv(2),
socket(7) SO_RCVTIMEO, sigsuspend(2).

Four blocked-call modes, each a variant of the blocked-* family (attach to
a target asleep in the call; registers at the stop):
  blocked-timed-futex  FUTEX_WAIT with a 300 ms relative timeout. Predicted
                       at the attach stop: rax -516 (restart_syscall),
                       orig_rax 202; resumed, the wait runs out (-ETIMEDOUT).
  blocked-timed-recv   recv on a socket with SO_RCVTIMEO 300 ms. Predicted:
                       rax -4 (a timed socket wait returns -EINTR, never a
                       restart code), orig_rax 45.
  blocked-sigsuspend   rt_sigsuspend. Predicted: rax -514
                       (ERESTARTNOHAND), orig_rax 130; a SIGUSR1 then shows
                       the same rax at its delivery stop and the handler's
                       delivery ends the call with -EINTR.
  blocked-order        a PTRACE_SYSCALL tracee asleep in a pipe read gets
                       SIGUSR1. Predicted: first a syscall-exit stop
                       (rax -512, orig_rax 0), then the signal-delivery
                       stop (SIGUSR1, rax -512). PAX at 536c082e shows only
                       the signal stop (named in restart.txt).
PAX at 9a2109a4 (restart.txt: "keep PAX's own settling"): the timed futex
