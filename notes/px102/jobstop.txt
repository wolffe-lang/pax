px102 — group-stop with PTRACE_LISTEN, and ATTACH to a job-control-stopped
target: the prediction, written 2026-11-06 before either witness has run.
Sources: ptrace(2) ("Group-stop", PTRACE_LISTEN, PTRACE_SEIZE, "Attaching and
detaching"), signal(7), wait(2).

group-stop: SEIZE a child that is not traced otherwise, SIGSTOP it.
Predicted: a PTRACE_EVENT_STOP report with SIGSTOP (status 0x80137f), siginfo
SIGSTOP; PTRACE_LISTEN keeps it stopped and wait sees nothing; SIGCONT then
brings a PTRACE_EVENT_STOP report with SIGTRAP (0x80057f); CONT resumes it
and the child exits 37.
attach-stopped: SIGSTOP a child, wait(WUNTRACED) sees 0x137f; ATTACH it.
Predicted: the attach is accepted and the tracer is told of a stop (0x137f)
though the task never ran; resuming it leaves it job-control-stopped until
SIGCONT; nothing else is predicted: the transcript is the reference.
PAX: PTRACE_LISTEN is "refused by name" (-EIO, a log line); no row separates
a group-stop from a signal-delivery stop. Expected RED at both modes.

Measured on Linux (three byte-identical takes per mode as uid 0 and 1000,
uid 0 equal to uid 1000): END linux-group-stop-0/-1000 and
linux-attach-stopped-0/-1000 exit=0 takes=3. References:
tests/mpx5-trace.d/group-stop.linux and attach-stopped.linux. Differences
from the prediction, recorded: the first witness's LISTEN after a SEIZEd
tracee's SIGSTOP signal-delivery stop (0x137f) is -EIO: only a real
group-stop may be listened to. The group-stop is entered by resuming that
stop with the signal injected (CONT 19): then 0x80137f, siginfo code 32787
(EVENT_STOP<<8|SIGSTOP... as 0x8013), LISTEN is 0 and the tracee stays
stopped, SIGCONT reports 0x80057f (code 32773), and resuming ends with the
SIGCONT's own signal-delivery stop 0x127f. ATTACH of a stopped process is
accepted with a 0x137f stop whose GETSIGINFO is -EINVAL (a group-stop has
no siginfo); CONT then reports 0x137f once more; SIGCONT is not reported.

Red on fbdc2b59 (both modes, by name): FAIL T-group-stop-event,
T-ptrace-listen, T-attach-stopped-attach, T-attach-stopped-resume. PAX
reports no group-stop to a tracer: a SEIZEd tracee resumed with SIGSTOP
injected goes into PAX's job-control stop and the tracer's later requests
are -ESRCH; ATTACH of a stopped process was accepted and then nothing was
reported.

R-px102-4 (recommendation: accept the refusal, implement later if sniff
asks). Contained fix found: ATTACH and SEIZE of a job-control-stopped
process is refused by name (-EIO and the log line "ptrace: refused attach of
a job-control-stopped process"); the rest is not contained: reporting a
group-stop to a tracer (PTRACE_EVENT_STOP with the stop signal, GETSIGINFO
absent, LISTEN keeping the stop, SIGCONT's notification) needs the
job-control park in kernel/sched (STOPPED, px48's wstop/wcont reports) to
hand a tracer the stop instead of the parent, and a resume path that leaves
it parked. group-stop.pax and attach-stopped.pax hold PAX's present
transcripts, so a change is seen. What sniff loses: the debugger cannot be
told that its inferior was stopped by SIGSTOP/SIGTSTP (Ctrl-Z) or attach to
a stopped process; ordinary signal-delivery stops, breakpoints, and
attach to a running process (S2 through S6) are unaffected.
